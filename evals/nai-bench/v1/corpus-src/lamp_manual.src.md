# Lumina LX2 LED desk lamp – User manual

Model LX2-W (white) and LX2-B (black). Manufacturer: Lumina Lighting Ltd., Unit 4, Mill Road, Leeds, UK.

## Technical data

| Property | Value |
|---|---|
| Rated power | 9 W |
| Luminous flux | 650 lm |
| Colour temperature | 2700 K to 6500 K (5 steps) |
| Energy efficiency class | F (on a scale from A to G) |
| Lifetime | 25,000 hours |
| USB charging port | 5 V, 1 A |

## Use

Touch the base once to switch on, hold to dim. The lamp remembers the last brightness. The timer switches
the lamp off after 45 minutes if the moon symbol is lit.

## Care and safety

Clean with a dry cloth only. Do not cover the lamp head while it is on. The LED cannot be replaced; at the
end of its life take the whole lamp to an electrical waste collection point.

## Warranty

Lumina gives a 2-year warranty from the date of purchase. Keep your receipt. Contact:
support@lumina-lighting.example.
