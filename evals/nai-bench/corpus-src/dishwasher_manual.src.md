# Aurora SilentWash SW600 – User Manual

Built-in dishwasher, 60 cm, 14 place settings. Read this manual before first use and keep it for future reference.

## 1. Before first use

Fill the salt container with about 1.5 kg of dishwasher salt and set the water hardness on the control panel (menu P3, levels H1–H7). Your local water supplier can tell you the water hardness. Use only detergent made for household dishwashers.

## 2. Programmes

| Programme | Temperature | Duration | Use |
|---|---|---|---|
| Eco 50 | 50 °C | 3:45 h | Normally soiled dishes, lowest energy use |
| Auto | 45–65 °C | 2:10–2:50 h | Sensor adjusts to the load |
| Intensive 70 | 70 °C | 2:35 h | Pots and pans, heavily soiled |
| Quick 35 | 35 °C | 0:35 h | Lightly soiled, no drying |
| Glass 40 | 40 °C | 1:50 h | Delicate glasses |

## 3. Error codes

| Code | Meaning | What you can do |
|---|---|---|
| E09 | Heating element fault | Restart the programme. If the code returns, call customer service. |
| E15 | Water in the base pan (leak protection has switched off the inlet) | Switch off and unplug the appliance, close the water tap, tilt the dishwasher carefully to the back to drain the base pan, check inlet and drain hoses for leaks. |
| E24 | Drain blocked | Clean the filter combination at the bottom of the tub, make sure the drain hose is not kinked, check the siphon connection. |
| E27 | Power supply too low | Check the mains voltage; contact an electrician. |

If an error code is not listed here, write down the code and contact customer service.

## 4. Cleaning and care

Clean the filter combination once a week. Run Intensive 70 without dishes once a month to remove grease and limescale.

## 5. Warranty

The manufacturer's warranty for the SilentWash SW600 is 24 months from the date of purchase. If you register the appliance at aurora-home.example/register within 8 weeks of purchase, the warranty is extended to 5 years for parts (labour is not included in the extension). Keep your proof of purchase.
