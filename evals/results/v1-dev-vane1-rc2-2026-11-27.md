# Vane 1 rc2 (first full run on Granite R2 97m) on nai-bench v1 dev – 2026-11-27

**PROVISIONAL.** v1 **dev split only** (47 answerable questions), no item human-reviewed, one run each. The 95 %
margin on MRR is roughly ±0.09; per-language and per-category groups (6–14 questions) are anecdotes.

- **rc2** = `ibm-granite/granite-embedding-97m-multilingual-r2` fine-tuned on the round-9/10 Vane 1 data:
  61,847 pairs after `--max-positive-rank 49` (real public passages 21,166, synthetic 30,170 + 10,511; see
  [the data plan](../../docs/lab/vane1-data-plan.md)), config
  [`training/configs/vane1-rc2-granite.json`](../../training/configs/vane1-rc2-granite.json): seed 13, 1 epoch,
  batch 64, lr 2e-5, max length 256, one mined negative where mining found one, in-batch negatives otherwise.
  58,791 training rows, 919 steps, about 3 minutes on the RX 7900 XTX. Saved at `training/runs/vane1-rc2/model`,
  exported to ONNX + int8 for fastembed (min cosine vs PyTorch 1.0000). Local only, not published.
- **Held-out pairs** (5 %, 3,056 pairs, same distribution as training): nDCG@10 0.735 → 0.801, MRR@10 0.668 →
  0.748, Hit@1 0.539 → 0.632, Hit@5 0.849 → 0.905.
- **Harness:** as for [rc1](v1-dev-vane1-rc1-2026-11-24.md): `evals/embeddings/dense_eval.py` over nAI's chunks of
  the v1 corpus (best chunk per file); hybrid = file-level RRF (k = 60) with nAI's BM25 order. Full table:
  `training/runs/vane1-pilot/eval/scores-rc2.md` (local).

## Reading

- **No measurable gain on v1 dev.** Every difference is inside the ±0.09 margin. 32× the pilot data moved the
  held-out pairs clearly (nDCG@10 +0.066) but not nai-bench: what rc2 learned is the training distribution
  (EU/DE/SE/US official texts and synthetic NAS documents written by one teacher), and v1 dev is a different one.
- **Cross-language Hit@5 is the one row that moved, downwards** (13 → 10 of 14). It is three questions, so not
  evidence of harm, but it is the row to watch, all the more because 53.6 % of the training pairs (33,120) are
  already cross-language: more of the same kind is unlikely to be the fix.
- **Not a release candidate.** Plain Granite stays nAI's default; nothing is published.
- Open questions, not started (decisions for the human): whether a second teacher adds the variety one teacher
  lacks, and whether v1 dev (47 questions) can show a gain of the size the held-out pairs show at all; a larger
  dev split would answer the second.
